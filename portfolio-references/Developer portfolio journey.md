# Developer portfolio: user journey

Source: Developer portfolio details.md, and the newer Portfolio proposal.md where the two differ. Journey from: an interview with Wan Zayd Abdullah on 23 September 2026.

This is a future-state journey. Every Feels line is a Positive target the design and content must deliver. Every Thinks and Feels line came from a recommendation Wan Zayd accepted, so each one carries "(suggested)".

## Personas

### Hiring manager

- **Who**: A manager who decides whether Wan Zayd gets an interview, often from outside low-code.
- **Goal**: Decide whether to interview him.
- **Context**: Opens the link from his LinkedIn or CV while reviewing a shortlist at work, on a desktop. Reads for a few minutes. Cannot judge the title "OutSystems Technical Lead" on its own.

### Technical lead

- **Who**: An interviewer on the panel who knows OutSystems.
- **Goal**: Judge his decisions and prepare interview questions.
- **Context**: Reads the page on a desktop before the interview. Reads the Main Projects closely. Knows low-code and system design.

### Recruiter

- **Who**: A recruiter who screens candidates against a job spec.
- **Goal**: Check his fit, then contact him.
- **Context**: Arrives from his LinkedIn, on a desktop or a phone. Spends one to two minutes. Uses his LinkedIn CV for any submission.

### Designer peer

- **Who**: A designer who sees the link shared somewhere, with no intent to hire.
- **Goal**: Judge the craft of the page.
- **Context**: Opens the page casually, often on a phone. Notices load speed and typography before reading.

## Journey: Hiring manager

### Stage 1: Arrive

- **Goal**: Get a first sense of the candidate.
- **Steps**:
  1. The hiring manager opens the link from his LinkedIn profile or CV.
  2. The page loads at once.
- **Uses**: Load on a slow connection spec
- **Thinks**: "That loaded fast." (suggested)
- **Feels**: Positive, because the page appears at once, a first sign of care (suggested)
- **Pain points**: None found.
- **Opportunities**: None found.

### Stage 2: Scan

- **Goal**: Find out who he is and whether to read further.
- **Steps**:
  1. The hiring manager reads the About text and the title "OutSystems Technical Lead".
  2. The hiring manager scans the three Main Projects.
- **Uses**: About, Main Project, rules 1 and 4
- **Thinks**: "Row locking at 40,000 enrolments. That is real engineering, whatever the platform." (suggested)
- **Feels**: Positive, because a decision answers the low-code doubt before it forms (suggested)
- **Pain points**:
  1. The hiring manager can dismiss him as "not real code" when they see the platform name.
- **Opportunities**:
  1. The reader meets a decision, for example row locking at 40,000 enrolments, before the platform name.

### Stage 3: Read a Main Project

- **Goal**: Judge how he designs a system.
- **Steps**:
  1. The hiring manager reads one Main Project: problem, decision, trade-off, role and team, outcome and tools.
  2. The hiring manager reads the iZone rebuild outcome, "Deployed to live, not yet in use", with the load test figure.
- **Uses**: Main Project, rules 3, 5, 9 and 11
- **Thinks**: "Solid. I want to hear more about this." (suggested)
- **Feels**: Positive, because the problem, decision and trade-off give them a reason to interview (suggested)
- **Pain points**: None found.
- **Opportunities**: None found.

### Stage 4: Check career

- **Goal**: Confirm that his history backs up the projects.
- **Steps**:
  1. The hiring manager reads the Timeline Entries, newest first, with Clients under Employers.
  2. The hiring manager checks the Qualifications near the About text.
- **Uses**: Timeline Entry, Qualification, Mark, rules 2, 7 and 8
- **Thinks**: "Steady progression, banks and big clients." (suggested)
- **Feels**: Positive, because Maybank, PETRONAS, Sunway University and his certifications support the projects (suggested)
- **Pain points**: None found.
- **Opportunities**: None found.

### Stage 5: Get in touch

- **Goal**: Invite him to an interview.
- **Steps**:
  1. The hiring manager writes to him by email or on LinkedIn.
- **Uses**: Contact
- **Thinks**: "Worth an interview, I'll message him." (suggested)
- **Feels**: Positive, because contact needs no form (suggested)
- **Pain points**: None found.
- **Opportunities**: None found.

## Changes to details

- CV download: the details document requires a CV file under Contact. The newer proposal and this journey remove it. Recruiters take his CV from LinkedIn (Recruiter, stage 4).
- Recruiter persona: the details document names hiring managers, technical leads and design-aware readers. The Recruiter is a fourth reader (Recruiter, all stages).
- LinkedIn content: the recruiter relies on the LinkedIn CV. Wan Zayd must update LinkedIn so that its tools match rule 3 and drop React, AWS and .NET/C# (Recruiter, stage 4).
- Load test detail: the iZone rebuild outcome must state the sustained 20-minute window beside the 40,000 figure (Technical lead, stage 5).
- Decision before platform: the About text and each Main Project must put a decision before the platform name (Hiring manager, stage 2).
- iZone team size: the details document leaves it open. The newer proposal settles it as three developers, Wan Zayd included (Technical lead, stage 2).
